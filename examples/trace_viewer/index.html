Answer summary

The KEYTRUDA (pembrolizumab, intravenous) label lists two melanoma indications: treatment of unresectable or metastatic melanoma, and adjuvant treatment of Stage IIB, IIC, or III melanoma following complete resection in adults and pediatric patients 12 years and older. I answered from the KEYTRUDA label only. A separate KEYTRUDA QLEX (subcutaneous) label also appeared in the search and was not used for this answer.
